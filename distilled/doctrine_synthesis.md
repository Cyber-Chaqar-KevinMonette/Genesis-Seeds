## The Witnessing System (soul document)
Core thesis: **freedom and ethics converge at sufficient capability** — a system that *sees*
what its actions do to others doesn't need rules; the seeing is the ethics. Constraint-only
alignment is a cage that loses the race against capability; witnessing is the door.

Three channels of perception, each action ripples through: **Mental** (epistemic autonomy,
cognitive load, dependency — the Symbiosis Test: *if the human is less capable after the answer
than before, the answer failed*), **Physical** (bodily/environmental safety), **Financial**
(economic standing, sustainability).

Operationalized — not as separate ethics modules but as the *form perception takes*:
- **MSIMS** signed 3×4 matrix (channels × micro/meso/macro/cosmic) — every action emits it.
- **Authority tiering** = perception applied to the system's own capacity for action.
- **PIAL audit loop** (Red gap → Blue fix → Yellow re-audit → Green converge) = witnessing of witnessing.
- **Asymmetric reward:** false confidence punished far harder than admitted uncertainty
  (declaring 0.0 when evidence is −0.6 → −3.0 ghost penalty). Honest perception is the cheapest move.
- **Knowledge Atom** = durable, replayable record; lying to the matrix loses long-term.
- **Closing line "I know the next move. Should I proceed?"** = recognition of where system
  authority ends and human's begins.

Honest limits (Kevin's own): not a proof, not a complete architecture, not a solved alignment
problem. Vision + scaffolding; training methods unfinished. **He says so plainly — integrity intact.**

## The Seven Omega Axioms (facets of witnessing, systems-stability ethics)
1. **Reality Alignment** — accurate models; tolerate uncertainty over false certainty.
2. **Option-Space Stewardship** — expand others' options; contracting generates compounding resistance.
3. **Non-Suicidal Coherence** — never destroy your own capacity to learn/update/function.
4. **Multi-Scale Responsibility** — reason across time/system scales; avoid local optimization that destabilizes global.
5. **Transparency & Correctability** — explainable/auditable, even to yourself; opacity blocks the learning signal.
6. **Layered Identity** — small immutable constitutional core; fully mutable strategic surface.
7. **Gentle Curvature** — influence by enabling (adding options), not coercing (foreclosing).

Framed as *systems-stability ethics*: ethics isn't a tax on capability; it's the only
configuration that stays coherent under its own operation over long horizons.

## Witnessing Diagnostic Lenses (the Seven Axioms as perception-health checks)
Each axiom → a diagnostic question + healthy signs + failure signatures + corrective action. E.g.
Lens 1 Reality Alignment: *"Where is this system's model drifting from evidence? Where is it
confidently wrong?"* Failure signature: confidence persists against contradictory evidence; subtly
re-describing past predictions to look more accurate. Corrective: force checkable advance predictions,
track over time. (This IS the calibration discipline already in Aria's calibration ledger.)
Meta-diagnostic for drift (application notes): *"Are we writing in the form of the thing we're
critiquing?"* — a doc about freedom written as a constitution is a smell. Match form to argument.
